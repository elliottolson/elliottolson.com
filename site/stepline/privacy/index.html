Stepline Privacy Policy

Stepline is an Apple Watch complication that compares today’s steps with your usual pace. Your data stays on your watch.

What Stepline reads

With your permission, Stepline reads your step count from Apple Health: today’s steps and the steps from the previous 28 days, which it averages into your usual pace. It reads nothing else from Health and never writes to it.

Where it goes

Nowhere. Stepline works out the numbers on your Apple Watch and keeps a small summary there so the complication can show it. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your Health data is never sent to me or to anyone else.

Your control

You can stop Stepline reading your steps at any time in the Health settings on your iPhone or watch. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.
